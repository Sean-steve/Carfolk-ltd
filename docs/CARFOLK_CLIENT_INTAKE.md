# Carfolk LTD — Client information and launch approval checklist

Collect the following information DIRECTLY from Carfolk. None of it should be invented or sourced from generic web search.

## Company identity and contacts
- Approved trading/legal name, tagline and logo usage.
- Domain ownership and preferred public HTTPS URL.
- Official business phone, WhatsApp, email, location pin/address and opening hours.
- Approved company/about text, actual services and statements the company can substantiate.

## Vehicle inventory
For every vehicle: unique internal stock reference, make, model, year, selling price KES, status, mileage, fuel type, transmission, body type, condition, engine capacity (if known), color, location, description, and approved photos with usage rights.
Optional: vehicle features and trim/variant. Do not publish unsupported inspection/warranty claims.

## Legal, data and operations
- Written privacy notice covering data controller identity, purposes, consent, retention, contact requests and deletion/review procedure.
- Named privacy/security contact and authorized dealership admin users.
- Approval of online enquiry and viewing request language; confirm that online payments are out of scope.
- Confirm hours/appointment rules, lead-response ownership and spam-reporting/incident escalation.

## Final launch evidence
- Approved screenshots on desktop and mobile with real vehicles and branding.
- Confirmed SSL/DNS/registrar control and backup restoration proof.
- Go-live approval for lead collection and separate approval for search indexing.
- Completed user acceptance testing, release summary and commercial milestone acceptance.

**Do not enter real customer PII in the public GitHub Pages preview.**
