# Model A — signed-scope baseline (KES 40,000)

## Included
Premium responsive homepage; catalogue; detailed vehicle pages; gallery and specification table; search and filters; featured/recent listings; available/reserved/sold statuses; browser-side comparison of up to three vehicles; financing **estimate**, not underwriting; direct WhatsApp/call; vehicle enquiry and test-drive request forms; About and Contact; privacy information.

A single secure administrator manages vehicles, photos, availability, featured selection, basic business information, enquiries and appointment requests.

Technical SEO baseline: semantic markup, meaningful metadata, appropriate structured data, image optimization and sitemap. Initial upload allowance: **up to 20 client-supplied vehicle records**. Post-launch bug fixes: **30 days**, under contract.

## Excluded unless separately contracted
Multiple dealerships/sellers, public user accounts, online purchase/deposit checkout, M-PESA, automatic loan approval, NTSA or bank integrations, full sales CRM, automated WhatsApp messages, native apps, professional photography, substantial catalogue migration, ongoing SEO/advertising, hosting and third-party fees.

## Quality rules
No fake stock, prices, reviews, dealership tenure or statistics. Public vehicle status and contact details must match client-approved facts. Customer PII must be protected. Financial estimates must be labelled illustrative.
